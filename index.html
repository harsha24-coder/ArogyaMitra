🧠

తెలుగు NLP + Triage

Prototype logic is rule-based. A production system would require clinical validation, safety controls, privacy safeguards and approved medical workflows.

1️⃣

Understand

User describes symptoms in Telugu or English.

2️⃣

Ask

Bot asks duration, severity and associated symptoms.

3️⃣

Triage

Rule-based demo assigns self-care, PHC or emergency guidance.

4️⃣

Care

Shows simple food, rest and escalation guidance.

5️⃣

Connect

Points users toward nearby public healthcare options.

6️⃣

Remember

Saves a local demo history for family/ASHA-assisted tracking.

🚨 Emergency Help

If this is a serious or life-threatening emergency, seek emergency care immediately.

Prototype note: verify local emergency/PHC contact details before deployment.
